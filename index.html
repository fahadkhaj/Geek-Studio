About the Studio

We are the visual voice
of East Africa's boldest brands.

Geek Studio is a full-service creative and digital agency born in Arusha. Founded by Fahad Khaj Mwinyi, we blend strategic thinking with obsessive craftsmanship — building brands, films, and digital systems that don't just look good. They move markets.

From the foothills of Kilimanjaro to global screens, we build identities with intention, campaigns with precision, and experiences with soul.
