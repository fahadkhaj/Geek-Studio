About the Studio

The visual voice of
East Africa's boldest brands.

Geek Studio is a full-service creative-tech ecosystem born in Arusha, Tanzania. Founded by Fahad Khaj Mwinyi, we blend strategic thinking with obsessive craftsmanship — building brands, films, and digital systems that drive real business growth.

From the slopes of Kilimanjaro to the shores of Zanzibar and the boardrooms of Dar es Salaam — we create identities, campaigns, and platforms that put African businesses on the global stage.
