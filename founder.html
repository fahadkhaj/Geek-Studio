Founder & Creative Technologist

Fahad Khaj Mwinyi.

"We're not just designing logos. We're designing the infrastructure for East Africa's next generation of businesses."

Fahad founded Geek Studio with a singular belief: African businesses deserve world-class creative and technical infrastructure — not as separate services, but as one integrated ecosystem.

With deep expertise spanning brand identity, cinematic production, web systems, and startup architecture, Fahad has shaped Geek Studio into Tanzania's most ambitious creative-tech company.

The studio's work with brands like Nova, Saadka Logistics, Africab, and Nunua Groceries demonstrates a rare ability to bridge creative excellence with technical depth.

When not directing brand systems or architecting SaaS platforms, Fahad explores the intersection of AI, automation, and African futurism — always asking what's next for business on the continent.
