The Visionary

Fahad Khaj Mwinyi.

Founder · Creative Director · Filmmaker

Fahad is the creative engine behind Geek Studio Tanzania. A multidisciplinary designer and filmmaker based in Arusha, he founded the studio in 2021 with a singular belief: great visual systems transcend trends and build lasting value for businesses.

With deep roots in brand strategy, cinematic videography, and digital experience design, Fahad has shaped identities for over 120 brands across East Africa — from Dar es Salaam startups to international tourism campaigns.
